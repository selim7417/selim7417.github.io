How a visit flows

From booking to receipt, in four steps.

Book

Double-click a free slot. You can add a new patient right there without losing the booking.

Treat & record

Record each procedure on the tooth you treated, with your notes.

Mark as done

Click Validate to confirm the visit happened and record when.

Invoice & receipt

The invoice and treatment list are filled in from the visit. Each payment gets its own receipt.

Prices are locked on the day of treatment. If you change your price list later, old invoices and treatment lists still show the price from the day the work was done.

Documents

Printed on your own letterhead.

Create a document and print it, save it as a PDF, or email it to the patient. Each patient's documents are saved to their own folder, so nothing gets mixed up.

Fully offline

The app works with no internet connection. You only need internet when you send an email.

Locked by password

A password unlocks the app, and you can lock it whenever you step away.

Scheduled backups

Snapshots are saved on a schedule. You can also back up by hand at any time, and restore when you need to.

Updates on your terms

Nothing downloads or installs until you say so. The app backs up all your data before any update installs.

Updates can't lose data

Every database change is tested against each past version before release, and each row is checked so nothing is lost.

Forgiving by default

A deleted patient is archived, not erased, and can be restored with one click.
